About Lotus Scout

Rooted in insight, built for growth.

We're a search firm built on a simple idea: the best hires come from deeply understanding both the role and the market, then doing the work to find people other firms miss.

The name

Why a lotus?

A lotus grows from deep roots and rises above the surface. That's how we see a great hire: someone grounded in real experience who grows into the role and lifts the people around them.

The tie at the center of our mark stands for the people we place. "Scout" is the job: going out, looking closely, and coming back with the right answer.

Founder

Adam Heugel

Adam founded Lotus Scout to bring a focused, insight-driven approach to executive and specialist search. He also founded Verde Talent, a careers platform and labor-market intelligence source for the energy industry, so Lotus Scout starts with an inside view of one of the fastest-changing talent markets in the country.

At Lotus Scout, Adam leads searches across executive leadership, management, IT, engineering, and sales in energy, technology, and beyond.
