Engagement options

Choose the model that fits the hire

Every engagement starts with a scoping call. We'll recommend the model that makes sense, which isn't always the most expensive one.

Retained Executive Search

For C-suite, VP, and other critical leadership roles. You get a dedicated, exclusive search with market mapping, a structured candidate slate, and regular progress reports.

Contingency Recruiting

For management, technical, engineering, and sales roles. You pay only when you hire, and you still get candidates we've screened properly.

Team & Project Hiring

For multiple hires: a new office, a product launch, or scaling a sales or engineering team. One partner and one process, with pricing for the full scope.

Our process

What working with us looks like

Discovery

An in-depth intake with you and key stakeholders on scope, must-haves, culture, compensation, and timeline.

Market mapping

We build a target list of companies and people and run direct, personal outreach. Posting a job ad is not the strategy.

Shortlist

Screened, interested candidates with written summaries covering experience, motivations, and compensation expectations.

Offer & onboarding

We coordinate interviews, run references, help close the offer, and check in after the start date.
